The factory kit keeps the double window, matching wall, cornice and pilaster from the 1K export. The forest uses the pine twig colour and opacity atlas on custom branch geometry. Brick and forest floor use 2K colour with 1K OpenGL normals and packed occlusion, roughness and metalness. Model placement and room layouts are specific to Saltline.
